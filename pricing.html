Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. You'll continue to have access until the end of your billing period.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell your data to third parties. We're SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.

What happens after my free trial?

After 14 days, your trial ends. You can upgrade to a paid plan to keep your data and continue using Orbitly, or downgrade to the free Starter plan.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any charges or credits.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes. If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days of your paid subscription, contact us for a full refund. No questions asked.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover) and PayPal. Studio plans can be invoiced annually.

Is there a discount for nonprofits?

Yes! We offer a 50% discount for registered nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact us with your proof of status.

Can I try Team features before upgrading?

Yes! Your 14-day trial includes all Team features. Invite your colleagues to try it out before committing to a paid plan.
