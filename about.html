Our Story

Orbitly started when three friends—a freelance designer, a developer, and a project manager—got tired of juggling five different apps just to run their work. Calendars, to-do lists, invoices, and client messages all lived in different places, and something always slipped through.

One evening over coffee, they sketched out what their ideal workspace would look like: one place where all the pieces fit together naturally. No more switching between tabs, no more forgotten invoices, no more "which app was that in again?"

Six months later, they had built the first version of Orbitly. They shared it with a few other freelancer friends, who loved it so much they insisted on paying for it. That feedback loop—building features that real people actually need—became the foundation of how we work.

Today, Orbitly helps over 10,000 freelancers and small teams stay organized and get paid on time. We're still a small, independent team, and we're still building features based on requests from our community.
