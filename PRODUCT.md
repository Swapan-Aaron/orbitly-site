## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (vanilla). Designed to be scalable — future backend integration planned.

## Users

Freelancers and small teams (2-15 people) who juggle multiple apps for calendars, tasks, invoices, and client communication. They need organized workflows without enterprise complexity or cost.

Secondary: Potential clients evaluating Orbitly through the marketing site to understand features, pricing, and brand before signing up.

## Product Purpose

Orbitly consolidates scheduling, project management, time tracking, client portals, and invoicing into one lightweight platform. It gives independent professionals the organized workflow that large companies enjoy, without the learning curve or price tag.

Success means visitors understand what Orbitly does, see its value for their workflow, and sign up for a trial.

## Positioning

The unified workspace built specifically for freelancers. Unlike general project tools (Asana, Trello) or invoicing-only apps (FreshBooks), Orbitly combines planning, execution, and payment in one place designed for solo work and small teams.

The Smart Planner automatically schedules work around meetings and energy levels — something freelancers do manually across multiple calendars.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from search, social media, or direct referrals
- They're evaluating whether to replace their current app stack
- Decision happens across multiple visits: features → pricing → trial
- Some check on mobile while commuting, others research at a desk
- The site must work as a standalone frontend with no backend (forms show success without sending)

## Product Principles

1. **Simplicity over features** — If a feature takes more than two minutes to learn, redesign it. Freelancers need tools that work immediately.

2. **Everything connects** — Scheduling, tasks, time tracking, and invoicing work together. No more context-switching between apps or manual data entry.

3. **Built with users, not assumptions** — Every major feature starts as a community request. We solve real workflow problems, not hypothetical ones.

4. **Transparent and fair** — Clear pricing, no hidden fees, 30-day money-back guarantee. Privacy-first: data is encrypted and never sold.

5. **Professional without the enterprise tax** — Small teams deserve organized workflows without paying for features built for 500-person companies.
